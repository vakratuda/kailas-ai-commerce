# Kailas AI Commerce

An AI-powered e-commerce platform for selling physical and digital products, creating marketing content, and managing online stores

Online store builder, physical and digital product management, AI-powered content creation, AI advertising and marketing, social media management, shopping cart and checkout, order and payment management, sales analytics with charts and graphs, marketing automation.

## Planned Features

- Online store builder
- Physical and digital product management
- AI-powered content creation
- AI advertising and marketing
- Social media management
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Order and payment management
- Sales analytics with charts and graphs
- Marketing automation

  ## Technology Stack

 HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, SQLite

 ## Project Status

Development is in progress. More features will be added in future updates.
